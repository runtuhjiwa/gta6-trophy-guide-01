Your progress never leaves this browser — it is saved in localStorage, and export/import moves it between devices.

How to use it

  1. Play the story first — but do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation right away (see the red warning below). Keep a manual save at the start of each chapter; there is no mission replay.
  2. Tick trophies off as you earn them — the checklist, filters and spoiler setting remember where you left off.
  3. Click any trophy — its guide, the earliest point it can be earned, and its source open beside the list.

    Roadmap to the platinum

    Four stages, in order. Stage 1 is the story; stages 2–3 are side content and the open world; stage 4 is the 100% cleanup after the credits. Every trophy sits in exactly one stage, and each card shows how many of its trophies you have earned.

    ⚠ The one trophy you can miss: Positive Mental Attitude. The first meditation is at Jason’s house in Chapter 1 — after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose “Accept”. It disappears after Chapter 1. Do it, then meditate at the other 17 spots. (PowerPyx)
